## Sources and probes

- Messages DIVIDE BY 0 and SQRT(NEG), and what X holds after C: keyrun --sequence at 867ddd5,
  quoted (kind message, kind value) so the check reads them on the device.
- A program stopped by an error leaves program entry at the line that refused it (R002 1/x,
  S004 √x); a run to the end leaves it at the top (PRGM TOP, as in fn-01). Probed, and pinned by
  %PC expectations (D04, D05, S05, S08).
- **The trap, found by the vectors themselves.** The first chain entered S after R's DIVIDE BY 0. E01
  (r(−2)) then gave −2.5, not −0.5: S's lines had gone into R after its 1/x, so R ran 1/(−2) on
  into S's 2 −. The lesson now enters both programs first, and teaches the trap. The claim "its
  lines would have gone into R, after the 1/x" is held by a side vector that is not in the lesson
  (the checker requires every lesson vector to have a keys block, and a student should not key this
  one in):

  `W01 | ... | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN PRGM 0 XEQ:R C PRGM LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 2 CHS XEQ:R C | ERR X=-2.5`

  Run with the firmware's vector runner at 867ddd5: 1/1. It is not re-run by `make check`; re-run
  it by hand at a repin.
- GTO . . (move the pointer to the top) is not taught: at 867ddd5, GOLD GTO . leaves the prompt
  at GTO _ and the second . is not taken (probe). The lesson uses the run-to-the-end route instead.
- Letters (keymap.c LETTER table): R on XEQ, S on 7. 1/x is the fifth key of the top row (as
  num-04 says) and √x the first.
