# eq-01 equations: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts MODE33
# FIX4 (the lesson's setup). EQN toggles Equation mode; RCL:v types a variable; = is the bar's = key.
Q01 | typing 2x + 3 = 11 and storing it | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER | %EQ=2×X+3=11
Q02 | continues Q01: XEQ evaluates it, first asking for X | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ | %STOP=INPUT:X
Q03 | continues Q02: try 4; left minus right is 0, so 4 solves it; 4 is stored in X | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS | X=0 @X=4
Q04 | continues Q03: show it again, try 5; 2 x 5 + 3 is 13, two more than 11 | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS EQN XEQ 5 RS | X=2 @X=5
Q05A | continues Q04: the equation shown again, then SOLVE waits for its variable | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS EQN XEQ 5 RS EQN | %EQ=2×X+3=11
Q05 | continues Q05A: X, and SOLVE finds 4 | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS EQN XEQ 5 RS EQN SOLVE:X | X=4 @X=4
Q05C | continues Q05: the backspace key clears only SOLVE's view; 4 stays in X | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS EQN XEQ 5 RS EQN SOLVE:X BKSP | X=4 @X=4
Q06A | without the x sign: 2X + 3 = 11, typed as a new equation | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER | %EQ=2X+3=11
Q06 | continues Q06A: it cannot be worked out: SYNTAX ERROR, after R/S | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS | ERR
Q06B@33s,35s | continues Q06: C clears the message, leaving the 4 typed | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS C | ERR X=4
Q06A@STU | STU: 2X is 2 x X (decision 53): stored with the x | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER | %EQ=2×X+3=11
Q06@STU | continues Q06A: STU works it out at 4: left minus right, 0 | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 RCL:X + 3 = 11 ENTER XEQ 4 RS | X=0
