# rpn-03 the display: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts
# MODE33 FIX4 (the lesson's setup); a display key inside an example changes only what is shown.
# Exact values: Python decimal at 34 digits, half even (docs/evidence/rpn-03.md).
P00 | a display key leaves the stack as it was: after ENTER a typed number still replaces X (FIX, SCI, ENG, ALL are neutral, 33s p.B-4; abacus #4278) | MODE33 FIX4 1 ENTER 2 ENTER FIX2 7 | X=7 Y=2 Z=1
P01 | 2 divided by 3, at FIX 4 | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 / | X=0.6666666666666666666666666666666667
P02 | the same number at FIX 2 | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 / FIX2 | X=0.6666666666666666666666666666666667
P03 | FIX 2 rounds only the display: times 3 gives exactly 2 | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 / FIX2 3 * | X=2
P04 | SCI 3: one digit before the point, three after, and a power of ten | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 / SCI3 | X=0.6666666666666666666666666666666667
P05 | ENG 3: powers of ten in steps of three | MODE33 FIX4 23456 ENG3 | X=23456
P06 | at FIX 4 a number too small for four places switches to scientific form (FIX 4 pressed again, since a setting stays until changed) | MODE33 FIX4 FIX4 1 ENTER 80000 / | X=0.0000125
P07 | E types a power of ten: 4.5 E 9 is 4.5 billion, shown at SCI 2 | MODE33 FIX4 4.5 E 9 SCI2 | X=4500000000
P08 | ALL shows a number with as many digits as it needs | MODE33 FIX4 1 ENTER 8 / ALL | X=0.125
P08B | ALL is limited by the width of the line: two thirds fills it | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 / ALL | X=0.6666666666666666666666666666666667
