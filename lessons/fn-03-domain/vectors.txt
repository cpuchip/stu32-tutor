# fn-03 domain: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts MODE33
# FIX4 (the lesson's setup). One chain; from D02 on it holds deliberate errors, hence ERR.
P01A | program R: r(x) = 1/x (R003) | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN | %LINES=3 %PC=R003
P01B | continues P01A: program S: s(x) = square root of (x - 2) (S005) | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN | %LINES=8 %PC=S005
P01 | continues P01B: program entry off | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM | %LINES=8
D01 | continues P01: r(4) | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R | X=0.25
D02 | continues D01: r(0) is refused: DIVIDE BY 0 | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R | ERR
D03 | continues D02: C clears it, the 0 left in X | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C | ERR X=0
D04 | continues D03: program entry opens at the line that refused | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM | ERR %PC=R002
D05 | continues D04: program entry off, nothing changed | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM | ERR %LINES=8 %PC=R002
S01 | continues D05: s(6) | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S | ERR X=2
S02 | continues S01: s(2) = 0, the edge of the domain | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S | ERR X=0
S03 | continues S02: s(1) is refused: SQRT(NEG) | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S | ERR
S04 | continues S03: C clears it; X holds x - 2 = -1, the number the square root refused | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C | ERR X=-1
S05 | continues S04: program entry opens at the line that refused, S's square root | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM | ERR %PC=S004
S06 | continues S05: program entry off | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM | ERR %LINES=8
S07 | continues S06: s(6) run to its end | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S | ERR X=2
S08 | continues S07: after a run to the end, program entry opens at the top | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S PRGM | ERR %PC=TOP
S09 | continues S08: program entry off | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S PRGM PRGM | ERR %LINES=8
E01 | exercise 1, continues S09: r(-2) | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S PRGM PRGM 2 CHS XEQ:R | ERR X=-0.5
E02 | exercise 2, continues E01: s(2.25) = square root of 0.25 | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S PRGM PRGM 2 CHS XEQ:R 2.25 XEQ:S | ERR X=0.5
E03 | exercise 3, continues E02: s(1.99) is refused | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S PRGM PRGM 2 CHS XEQ:R 2.25 XEQ:S 1.99 XEQ:S | ERR
E03B | continues E03: C; X holds 1.99 - 2 | MODE33 FIX4 PRGM LBL:R INV RTN LBL:S 2 - SQRT RTN PRGM 4 XEQ:R 0 XEQ:R C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S 2 XEQ:S 1 XEQ:S C PRGM PRGM 6 XEQ:S PRGM PRGM 2 CHS XEQ:R 2.25 XEQ:S 1.99 XEQ:S C | ERR X=-0.01
