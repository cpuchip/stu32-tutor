# num-04 percent and powers of ten: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours.
# Each starts MODE33 FIX2 (the lesson's setup). Values: exact fractions (docs/evidence/num-04.md).
C00 | % leaves the stack where it was: 15% of 80 with 1 and 2 above | MODE33 FIX2 1 ENTER 2 ENTER 80 ENTER 15 PCT | X=12 Y=80 Z=2 T=1
C01 | 15% of 80: % works X percent of Y and keeps Y | MODE33 FIX2 80 ENTER 15 PCT | X=12 Y=80
C02 | continues C00: + adds the 12 to the 80 kept in Y | MODE33 FIX2 1 ENTER 2 ENTER 80 ENTER 15 PCT + | X=92 Y=2
C03 | 25% off 60: the discount, then subtract it | MODE33 FIX2 60 ENTER 25 PCT - | X=45
C04 | percent change from 50 to 65, Y kept | MODE33 FIX2 50 ENTER 65 PCTCHG | X=30 Y=50
C05 | percent change from 80 down to 60 | MODE33 FIX2 80 ENTER 60 PCTCHG | X=-25
C06 | a power of ten typed with a negative exponent: 4 E -3 | MODE33 FIX2 4 E 3 CHS ENTER | X=0.004
C06B | +/- before E makes the front number negative instead | MODE33 FIX2 4 CHS E 3 ENTER | X=-4000
C07 | 6 E 5 times 4 E -3 | MODE33 FIX2 6 E 5 ENTER 4 E 3 CHS * | X=2400
C09 | 9 E -6 divided by 3 E 2 | MODE33 FIX2 9 E 6 CHS ENTER 3 E 2 / | X=3E-8
C08 | 3.2 E 12 times 2.5 E 9: too long for the line at FIX 2, shown in scientific form | MODE33 FIX2 3.2 E 12 ENTER 2.5 E 9 * | X=8E21
