# poly-04 complex roots: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts
# MODE33 FIX4 (the lesson's setup). CI is the i that separates a complex number's parts (unit 009).
C01A | i typed: 0, then CMPLX i, then 1 (shown 0i1_) | MODE33 FIX4 0 CI 1 | X=0i1
C01 | continues C01A: i times i | MODE33 FIX4 0 CI 1 ENTER * | X=-1i0
C02 | 4i squared | MODE33 FIX4 0 CI 4 ENTER * | X=-16i0
V01 | the square root key refuses a complex number: INVALID DATA | MODE33 FIX4 16 CHS CI 0 SQRT | ERR
V01B | continues V01: C clears the message | MODE33 FIX4 16 CHS CI 0 SQRT C | ERR
V02 | x^2 refuses it too | MODE33 FIX4 0 CI 1 SQ | ERR
V02B | continues V02: C clears the message | MODE33 FIX4 0 CI 1 SQ C | ERR
R01 | x^2 + 2x + 5: (-2 + 4i) / 2 | MODE33 FIX4 2 CHS ENTER 0 CI 4 + 2 / | X=-1i2
R02 | (-2 - 4i) / 2 | MODE33 FIX4 2 CHS ENTER 0 CI 4 - 2 / | X=-1i-2
H01 | -1 + 2i in x^2 + 2x + 5 by Horner | MODE33 FIX4 1 CHS CI 2 ENTER ENTER ENTER 1 * 2 + * 5 + | X=0i0
H02A | -1 - 2i typed: +/- before i for the real part, after it for the i part (-1i-2_) | MODE33 FIX4 1 CHS CI 2 CHS | X=-1i-2
H02 | continues H02A: -1 - 2i in x^2 + 2x + 5 by Horner | MODE33 FIX4 1 CHS CI 2 CHS ENTER ENTER ENTER 1 * 2 + * 5 + | X=0i0
E01 | exercise: x^2 - 4x + 13: (4 + 6i) / 2 | MODE33 FIX4 4 ENTER 0 CI 6 + 2 / | X=2i3
E02 | (4 - 6i) / 2 | MODE33 FIX4 4 ENTER 0 CI 6 - 2 / | X=2i-3
E03 | 2 + 3i in x^2 - 4x + 13 by Horner | MODE33 FIX4 2 CI 3 ENTER ENTER ENTER 1 * 4 - * 13 + | X=0i0
