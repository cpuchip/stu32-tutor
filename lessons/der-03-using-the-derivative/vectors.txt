# der-03 using the derivative: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts
# MODE33 FIX4 (the lesson's setup). Values from build/proto/der03.py; the GRAPH tools are numeric
# (040: SOLVE's engine, a central difference), so their results carry a tolerance.
T01 | q(3.1) on the stack: 3.1 ENTER x^2 x<>y 4 x - 3 + | MODE33 FIX4 3.1 ENTER SQ XY 4 * - 3 + | X=0.21
T02 | the tangent at 3.1: 3.1 ENTER 2 x 6 - | MODE33 FIX4 3.1 ENTER 2 * 6 - | X=0.2
T03 | q(3.05): half the distance | MODE33 FIX4 3.05 ENTER SQ XY 4 * - 3 + | X=0.1025
S01 | SOLVE the derivative 2X - 4 = 0 (an expression: left minus right) | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 2 * RCL:X - 4 ENTER SOLVE:X | X=2 @X=2
S02 | q(2) on the stack | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER SQ XY 4 * - 3 + | X=-1
C01 | f(-1), f(x) = x^3 - 3x: -1 ENTER ENTER 3 y^x x<>y 3 x - | MODE33 FIX4 1 CHS ENTER ENTER 3 POW XY 3 * - | X=2
C02 | f(1) | MODE33 FIX4 1 ENTER ENTER 3 POW XY 3 * - | X=-2
G01@STU | STU: q typed, Equation mode off, fn-02's window | MODE33 FIX4 EQN RCL:X POW 2 - 4 * RCL:X + 3 ENTER EQN 2 CHS XMIN 5.98 XMAX | X=5.98
G02@STU | continues G01: GO, then five columns right: x = 2.1 | MODE33 FIX4 EQN RCL:X POW 2 - 4 * RCL:X + 3 ENTER EQN 2 CHS XMIN 5.98 XMAX GRAPH GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 | %GCUR=205 %GCXY=2.1,-0.99
G03@STU | continues G02: EXTR on the FCN page: x = 2, the trace moved there | MODE33 FIX4 EQN RCL:X POW 2 - 4 * RCL:X + 3 ENTER EQN 2 CHS XMIN 5.98 XMAX GRAPH GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 EXTR | %GRES#=2,1E-15 %GCUR=200
G04@STU | continues G03: SLOPE at the cursor: 0 (a central difference, exact on a parabola) | MODE33 FIX4 EQN RCL:X POW 2 - 4 * RCL:X + 3 ENTER EQN 2 CHS XMIN 5.98 XMAX GRAPH GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 EXTR GSLOPE | %GRES#=0,1E-15
G05@STU | continues G04: TANL: y = 0x - 1, flat | MODE33 FIX4 EQN RCL:X POW 2 - 4 * RCL:X + 3 ENTER EQN 2 CHS XMIN 5.98 XMAX GRAPH GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 EXTR GSLOPE TANL | %GRES#=0,1E-15 %GRESB#=-1,1E-15
G06@STU | continues G05: C leaves the graph | MODE33 FIX4 EQN RCL:X POW 2 - 4 * RCL:X + 3 ENTER EQN 2 CHS XMIN 5.98 XMAX GRAPH GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 GMOVE:6 EXTR GSLOPE TANL C | %GRAPH=0
