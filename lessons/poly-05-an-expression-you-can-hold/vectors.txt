# poly-05 an expression you can hold: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts MODE33
# FIX4 (the lesson's setup). EQN toggles Equation mode; EQDROP is EQN MORE's →X and EQSTORE its →EQN (082); BST is
# the list's ▲. %XEXPR and %YEXPR are X's and Y's expression as STU's text, none for a number; %XDRAWN is X as the
# screen draws it (unit 082, firmware 67db051; →X leaves Equation mode from unit 083).
# From before
PL5F1 | from before: 3x^2 - 4 at x = 2, typed | MODE33 FIX4 8 | X=8
PL5F1W | its working | MODE33 FIX4 2 SQ 3 * 4 - | X=8
PL5F1A | slip: squared the 3x | MODE33 FIX4 3 ENTER 2 * SQ 4 - | X=32
PL5F2 | from before: left minus right for 4x - 1 = 9 at 3, typed | MODE33 FIX4 2 | X=2
PL5F2W | its working | MODE33 FIX4 4 ENTER 3 * 1 - 9 - | X=2
PL5F2A | slip: right minus left | MODE33 FIX4 9 ENTER 4 ENTER 3 * 1 - - | X=-2
# The examples, each continuing the one before
B01 | typing 3x^2 - 4 | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER | %EQ=3×X^2-4 %XEXPR=none
B02 | continues B01: →X, Equation mode left, the expression in X | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP | %XEXPR=3×X^2-4 %XDRAWN=3X^2-4 %YEXPR=none %EQN=1
B03 | continues B02: ENTER copies it into Y | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER | %XEXPR=3×X^2-4 %YEXPR=3×X^2-4
N01 | continues B03: 2 x is NOT A NUMBER, nothing changed | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * | ERR X=2 %YEXPR=3×X^2-4
N02 | continues N01: C clears the message | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C | ERR X=2 %YEXPR=3×X^2-4
K01 | continues N02: x<>y, STO A | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C XY STO:A | ERR %XEXPR=3×X^2-4 Y=2
K02 | continues K01: C, RCL A brings it back | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C XY STO:A C RCL:A | ERR %XEXPR=3×X^2-4 %XDRAWN=3X^2-4
L01 | continues K02: x + 1 typed into the list | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C XY STO:A C RCL:A EQN RCL:X + 1 ENTER | ERR %EQ=X+1 %EQN=2 %XEXPR=3×X^2-4
L02 | continues L01: →EQN puts X's expression after x + 1 | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C XY STO:A C RCL:A EQN RCL:X + 1 ENTER EQSTORE | ERR %EQ=3×X^2-4 %EQN=3 %XEXPR=3×X^2-4
L03 | continues L02: the entry before | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C XY STO:A C RCL:A EQN RCL:X + 1 ENTER EQSTORE BST | ERR %EQ=X+1 %EQN=3
L04 | continues L03: C leaves, X kept its copy | MODE33 FIX4 EQN 3 * RCL:X POW 2 - 4 ENTER EQDROP ENTER 2 * C XY STO:A C RCL:A EQN RCL:X + 1 ENTER EQSTORE BST C | ERR %XEXPR=3×X^2-4 %XDRAWN=3X^2-4
