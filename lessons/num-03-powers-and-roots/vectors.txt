# MODE33 FIX4 (the lesson's setup). Values: exact integers and fractions (docs/evidence/num-03.md).
R01 | 3 squared | MODE33 FIX4 3 SQ | X=9
R02 | the square root of 49 | MODE33 FIX4 49 SQRT | X=7
R03 | 2 to the 10th: y^x raises Y to the power X | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 10 POW | X=1024
R04 | the order matters: 10 squared is 10 ENTER 2 y^x | MODE33 FIX4 10 ENTER 2 POW | X=100
R05 | the cube root of 27: x-root takes the X-th root of Y | MODE33 FIX4 27 ENTER 3 XROOT | X=3
R06 | a negative power: 2 to the -3 is 1/8 | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 CHS POW | X=0.125
R07 | a root as a power: 16 to the 1/2 is 4 | MODE33 FIX4 16 ENTER .5 POW | X=4
R07B | a third as a power, typed as a fraction: 27 to the 1/3 | MODE33 FIX4 27 ENTER .1.3 POW | X=3
R08 | (2 cubed) squared | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 POW 2 POW | X=64
R09A | inside R09, before the powers: 2 waits in Z, 3 in Y, 2 in X | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 ENTER 2 | X=2 Y=3 Z=2
R09B | continues R09A: the first y^x works 3 squared, leaving 9 in X and the 2 back in Y | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 ENTER 2 POW | X=9 Y=2
R09 | 2 to the (3 squared) | MODE33 FIX4 2 ENTER 3 ENTER 2 POW POW | X=512
R10 | 10 to the 3rd with 10^x | MODE33 FIX4 3 TENX | X=1000
R11 | the reciprocal of 8 with 1/x | MODE33 FIX4 8 INV | X=0.125
