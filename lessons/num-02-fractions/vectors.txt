# num-02 fractions: key vectors (the firmware's format). Every example is ours. Each starts MODE33
# FIX4 (the lesson's setup). A continuation's vector holds the keys of the examples it continues.
# Values: Python decimal at 34 digits, half even (docs/evidence/num-02.md).
G01 | a fraction typed: 3/8 is .3.8 | MODE33 FIX4 .3.8 ENTER | X=0.375
G02 | a mixed number: 2 3/8 is 2.3.8 | MODE33 FIX4 2.3.8 ENTER | X=2.375
G03 | Fraction display shows it as a fraction | MODE33 FIX4 2.3.8 ENTER FDISP | X=2.375
G07 | continues G03: the fraction key again goes back to FIX 4 | MODE33 FIX4 2.3.8 ENTER FDISP FDISP | X=2.375
G05 | 5 3/8 + 2 7/16 inches is 7 13/16, exactly | MODE33 FIX4 5.3.8 ENTER 2.7.16 + FDISP | X=7.8125
G06 | continues G05, Fraction display still on: 3/4 of 2/3 is 1/2 | MODE33 FIX4 5.3.8 ENTER 2.7.16 + FDISP .3.4 ENTER .2.3 * | X=0.5
G04 | continues G06: 1/2 + 1/3 is 5/6, stored a little below 5/6 | MODE33 FIX4 5.3.8 ENTER 2.7.16 + FDISP .3.4 ENTER .2.3 * .1.2 ENTER .1.3 + | X=0.8333333333333333333333333333333333
G08 | continues G04: the fraction key turns it off, back to FIX 4 | MODE33 FIX4 5.3.8 ENTER 2.7.16 + FDISP .3.4 ENTER .2.3 * .1.2 ENTER .1.3 + FDISP | X=0.8333333333333333333333333333333333
